§01 The Firm Halol, Panchmahal

Fourteen years, and our own trucks in the yard

Panchal Enterprise has traded industrial gas out of Halol since 2011 — oxygen, argon, nitrogen, carbon dioxide and dissolved acetylene, alongside bulk liquid oxygen in a vacuum-insulated cryogenic container. Buyers run from fabrication and manufacturing through to hospitals and medical facilities under our medical oxygen licence.

What separates us from a newer trading setup is logistics. We own our delivery vehicles and a fleet of 700+ cylinders. Many are in active circulation with regular clients at any moment; the rest are available for new orders. Large or repeat requisitions get met without waiting on a third-party carrier.

§03 Liquid Oxygen Cryogenic bulk

One tank instead of twenty-two trips

−183°C

Boiling point of liquid oxygen · vacuum-insulated container

For high-volume users, cylinders stop making sense. A single cryogenic container holds roughly 151 m³ of oxygen in liquid form — about the same as twenty-one and a half standard cylinders, in one delivery, with one connection to manage instead of a wall of valves.

Volume equivalence 1 tank ≈ 21.5 cylinders

1 × Cryo
151 m³

21.5 × standard O₂ cylinder · 7 CBM each

Why it matters: fewer deliveries, less handling, and far less floor space given over to cylinder storage. Liquid oxygen is stored and moved following standard cryogenic safety practice, and supplied to medical buyers under our licence.

      §07 Handling Read before use

      Before anything is connected

      Medical oxygen, under licence

      Oxygen supplied to hospitals, medical facilities and patients is provided under our medical oxygen licence, and should be used under the guidance of a qualified medical professional.

      Cryogenic handling

      Liquid oxygen sits at roughly −183°C in a vacuum-insulated container. It's stored and transported following standard cryogenic safety practice, by people trained for it.

      Checked before it leaves

      Every cylinder and container is checked before it's supplied onward. Store upright and secured, clear of heat and direct sun.

      Documentation on request

      If your procurement process needs specific safety documentation, say so when you enquire and we'll sort it.
